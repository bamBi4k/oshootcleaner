Oh Shoot is a one-button "refresh everything" app for Android that does only what a non-rooted app is actually allowed to do, no fake progress bars, no pretending to kill other apps' services.

WHAT IT DOES
• Clears its own cache, code cache, and the shared WebView cache
• Frees RAM by asking Android to evict cached background processes
• Reports real before/after free-RAM numbers
• Deep-links you straight to the Settings screens that can clear every other app's cache, show real battery drain, and limit background processes

PRESETS
Three performance presets (Power Saving, Balanced, Performance) that change brightness, screen timeout, auto-rotate, and Do Not Disturb. Some settings require a one-time manual grant via Android Settings; the app tells you clearly which ones.

HONEST LIMITS
Android's sandboxing means a normal app cannot delete another app's cache, force-stop another app's services, or list what's really running. This app doesn't pretend otherwise. It does what it can, and tells you where to go for the rest.

PRIVACY
No trackers, no analytics, no ads. The app requests no internet permission. Everything happens on-device.

FREE SOFTWARE
Oh Shoot is open source under the Apache 2.0 license. Source code is available on GitHub.
